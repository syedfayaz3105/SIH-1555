## Problem Title
SIH 1555: Create a Virtual Herbal Garden that provides an interactive, educational, and immersive experience to users, showcasing the diverse range of medicinal plants used in AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy).
## Problem Description
Background: The AYUSH sector relies heavily on medicinal plants and herbs, which form the backbone of traditional healing practices. However, physical gardens that are not accessible to everyone. A Virtual Herbal Garden will bridge this gap by offering a digital platform where users can explore, learn, and understand the significance of various medicinal plants from the comfort of their homes. Description: Participants are tasked with developing a Virtual Herbal Garden that is engaging, informative, and user-friendly. This virtual garden should include: Interactive 3D Models: Realistic 3D models of medicinal plants that users can rotate, zoom, and explore from different angles. Detailed Information: Comprehensive details about each plant, including its botanical name, common names, habitat, medicinal uses, and methods of cultivation. Multimedia Integration: High-quality images, videos, and audio descriptions to enhance the learning experience. Search and Filter Options: Advanced search functionality to easily locate specific plants and filter them based on various criteria like medicinal uses, region, and type. Virtual Tours: Guided virtual tours highlighting specific themes, such as plants for digestive health, immunity, skin care, etc. User Interaction: Features that allow users to bookmark favourite plants, take notes, and share information on social media. Expected Outcome: The expected outcome is a comprehensive Virtual Herbal Garden that serves as a valuable educational tool for students, practitioners, and enthusiasts of the AYUSH sector. This platform should make the knowledge of medicinal plants accessible to a wider audience, promoting awareness and understanding of traditional herbal practices. It should be visually appealing, informative, and interactive, providing users with an immersive experience that combines technology with traditional knowledge.

## Problem Statement Summary:
Design and develop a digital platform that provides an immersive experience to learn about medicinal plants used in AYUSH systems. The platform should include interactive 3D models, educational content, multimedia integration, and user interaction features.

## Proposed Solution Overview:
VedicVerde is a web-based and VR-compatible platform that simulates a fully immersive Virtual Herbal Garden. It combines cutting-edge 3D visualization with rich traditional knowledge of medicinal plants to serve as an educational resource for users across the globe.

## 1. Interactive 3D Models
Use WebGL/Three.js for plant visualization.

Rotate, zoom, and pan views of each plant.

Hover effects to display quick facts.

Compatible with both desktop and VR devices (Oculus, Google Cardboard).

## 2. Comprehensive Plant Information
Each plant’s profile includes:

Botanical name, common names in multiple languages.

Geographic distribution & habitat.

Ayurvedic classification (Rasa, Guna, Virya, Vipaka).

Medicinal uses (disease-wise applications).

Preparation methods (decoctions, pastes, infusions).

Cultivation guide (soil, climate, propagation, harvesting).

## 3. Multimedia Integration
High-resolution images for identification.

Short videos demonstrating preparation and application.

Voice narrations in English + regional languages for accessibility.

Audio-guided learning mode for visually impaired users.

## 4. Guided Virtual Tours
Themed virtual paths such as:

Immunity Boosters

Skin Health Allies

Respiratory Health

Stress Relief & Mental Wellness

Narrated by experts or AYUSH practitioners.

## 5. Advanced Search & Filter
Search by plant name, use case, region, AYUSH system.

Filters: toxicity level, plant type (tree, herb, shrub), seasonality.

Predictive suggestions with auto-complete.

## 6. User Interaction Features
Bookmark favorite plants for quick access.

Add personal notes to any plant profile.

Share plant info via social media (with QR or image cards).

Rate and comment on guided tours.

Quiz modules to reinforce learning.
